# MS10 evaluation rules (10d, amended at 10e; written before any network run)

Status: FROZEN with the MS10 lists at 10d (DL-308); amended at 10e (DL-314) when the reviewer ruled the ten evaluation choices E-1 to E-10 (R25 to R28, DL-310 to DL-313). Binding sources: `docs/plan.md` (Table 2.1, Table 7.2 RB1 and RB8, Tables 4.2 and 11.2 G4, 6.4 and CP21 for sigma), the frozen criteria (`configs/robustness_criteria.yaml`, tag `criteria-frozen`), and the rulings R1 to R28 in `docs/ms10_plan.md`. This file adds no criterion, changes no threshold and reopens no ruling. It says in words what the evaluators (`src/mprsurge/analysis/ms10_eval.py`, `scripts/evaluate_ms10.py`, tests in `tests/test_ms10_eval.py`) compute. The ruled choices are the values of `configs/ms10/ms10_eval.yaml`; a null or absent value still stops the evaluator with `MissingChoice`. Nothing here is simulated: the evaluators read stored records only. No network record was read before the 10e rulings.

## What every evaluator does first

1. The criteria loader gate (tag `criteria-frozen`) must verify; the RB1 and RB8 leaves are read from the tag's blob, not from the working file.
2. Any MS10 data file (lists, cells, references, eval choices, the two run configs) that is not FROZEN stops the evaluation.
3. **Time origins (R27).** Before any network record is read, the evaluator states and compares the time origin of the classifier onset (network) and of the mean-field onset: the same path clock (path time of sample 0 equal), the same `t_on`, the same sample interval. If they differ it stops (`EvalRefused`, "STOP (R27)"). Both modes classify on sample index over the sampling rate, v starting at t = 0 with the 2 s discard removed. Mean-field sample i is the state at t = i ms (first stamp 0 ms); network sample i is the mean over the 1 ms bin that ends at (i + 1) ms (first stamp 1 ms). The stamp convention differs by one sample (reported as `stamp_offset_ms` = 1.0); the classifier time, on which both onsets are defined, is i / fs in both. Each network record that carries `t_ms` is re-checked: its first stamp must be one bin.
4. Records are looked up by the rows of the frozen run lists, by name. A record is refused (the evaluation stops, nothing is skipped) if its git dirty flag is not exactly false, if it is a dry-run record, if its full config hash or its name differs from the row of the frozen list, or if its criteria SHA-256 is not the frozen one. A record in a family folder that is not a row of the frozen lists is refused. A listed record that is absent is reported as missing and the verdict of every part that needs it is INCOMPLETE; it is never replaced.
5. A record whose status is not ok is class `failed` (A9, never NS) and matches nothing.

## Classes and the mean-field comparator

The class of a network run is the frozen mode-N class of the network signal v (mean V over non-refractory neurons, 1 ms), with the window and step of rule D4 for its T_ramp and the 5 s baseline wholly in [0, t_on]. The mean-field comparator is the mode-D class of: the stored record for the headline at T_ramp 10 s and 120 s (`configs/ms10/mf_references.yaml`, SHA-256 verified; R13); the companion run for each S8 cell at 30 s; the companion run for the headline at 60 s (S9 and S10). **Same class** (primary) is the Table 2.1 correspondence: D-tr meets S+, S-rel or S-abs; D-sus meets S-sus; D-none meets NS. The second reading (post hoc, DL-258 b, R2) does not count S-rel as a match for D-tr; it is reported beside the primary one and feeds no gate. The limits DL-229 and DL-258 b apply to every network class; the C2 control classes (R17) are printed beside each ramp class (same N, T_ramp, shape, Delta and tau_d; 3 seeds) with no verdict.

## RB1 (R15, R18, R23, R26, R27)

*Headline, per T_ramp (10 s and 120 s), per seed (3 seeds).* A seed passes when (a) its class is the same class as the mean-field class, (b) its f_pk is within 5 percent of the stored mode-D f_pk (relative to the mean-field value; the frozen `f_pk_rel_tol`), and (c) its onset is within one spectrogram step of the mean-field onset (step W/2 for T_ramp below 60 s, 1 s from 60 s on; the frozen text). **Both tolerances are inclusive** (<=, E-3); the margin (tolerance minus the difference, zero or positive means met) is reported beside each test. **A seed whose f_pk or onset is undefined fails that test** and is reported "undefined" (E-2). If the mean-field reference has no f_pk or onset (not D-tr), there is nothing to compare, the test is reported "n/a" and counts as met; the class test carries the comparison (our reading, UNVERIFIED; it does not arise for the headline references, all D-tr).

*Network onset (R27, replaces the raw-crossing reading of R2 and R23 for network records).* The onset of a network run is the classifier onset: the centre of the first flagged spectrogram window (`Conventions.onset` default), the earlier of the relative and absolute episodes after the minimum-cycle and D8 tests. It is compared with the stored mode-D mean-field raw-crossing onset. The evaluator's own raw-crossing reading (first time the band-passed Hilbert envelope of the network v reaches 0.1 x A_ref after t_on, no cycle count, no D8) is **secondary and post hoc**: it is reported with its difference and its own within-one-step flag, feeds no gate, and may be undefined. The mode-D classification and the RB3 readings are unchanged.

f_pk is the Table 2.1 peak frequency of the classifier's first episode (argmax of the PSD in 25 to 100 Hz in its peak-power window), so its resolution is 1/W: 3 Hz at 10 s, 0.5 Hz at 120 s. The difference in Hz and in frequency bins is printed beside the test (R18); the test itself is the 5 percent as written. The headline passes at a T_ramp when 3 of 3 seeds pass (frozen `classes_agree_seeds`). The post-hoc median reading takes the median f_pk and the median onset of the three seeds against the mean field, with the three classes still required to match; it feeds no gate (R23).

*Cells (S8 map cells, 30 s).* A cell agrees only if all 3 seeds fall in the class that matches the companion mode-D class of that cell (R15). RB1 needs at least 3 of the 4 cells to agree (frozen `classes_agree_cells`). f_pk and onset are not tested for cells (R23 names them for the headline). The stored 120 s class of each cell is printed beside the companion class.

*N rule (R18).* From S9 (60 s, 10 seeds per N): the number of seeds in the mean-field-matching class at N = 3e4 must be at least the number at N = 1e4 (class-based, primary). The f_pk and onset errors (median and range over the seeds) are printed for every N and are not a pass test (frozen `no_worse_tolerance`).

*RB1 verdict.* PASS when both headline T_ramp pass, the cells pass and the N rule passes; FAIL when every part is in and one fails; INCOMPLETE otherwise. Failing RB1 is G4 (frozen `on_fail`).

## Sigma check (R16, R25, R28)

*Reference (R25, E-10).* The mean-field baseline std of v is the MS7 baseline-only record `results/S5/baseline/S5base_0000_1fe2ceaa1659` (headline cell, 7 s, 2 s discard, 10 seeds, spawn keys [7,4,0] to [7,4,9], sigma_used 0.0624, git dirty false, commit 87af6c19): the mean over the 10 seeds of the std of v over samples [0, 5000), ddof 1, 0.039522 (per seed 0.037612 to 0.041724). The DL-120 value 0.038852 (runs at sigma 0.0623888, git dirty true) is not used; no MF_SIGMA_REF list exists.

*Window (R28, E-4).* One common window for the reference and for every network baseline: samples [0, 5000) of v (the 5 s baseline of Table 2.1 at 1 kHz), std with ddof 1. The classifier's own baseline windows are not used for this check (at T_ramp 10 s they end at sample 4842).

*Test (E-5).* The network baseline std of each of the 6 S8 headline runs (3 seeds at 10 s, 3 at 120 s) is computed in that window. The reference must lie inside the range of those 6 values, **ends included** (min <= x <= max). The position of the reference in the range (0 at the minimum, 1 at the maximum) and the margin to the nearest end are reported. If it does not lie inside, it is reported as a limitation and sigma stays frozen (MS7); the evaluator never writes sigma. The six values are printed with the number of distinct values, overall and per seed across the two T_ramp: the baseline of a seed is expected to be identical at 10 s and 120 s because the parameters are constant before the ramp (UNVERIFIED; the evaluator reports it, it does not assume it).

## S9 (finite size; E-6)

Per N (1e3, 3e3, 1e4, 3e4): class counts, seeds in a surge class, seeds in the matching class (both readings), and the **Table 2.1 Metrics row** for each seed: class, onset, eta_bar at onset (the path value at the onset time), delay (eta_on minus the nominal eta_H), duration (ms, cycles, fraction of the ramp = duration / T_ramp), peak absolute power, peak relative power, peak frequency, and on P2 J_exit,run (J at the end of the classifier episode, when it terminates before the record end), J_exit,static (A4 reading of DL-209, UNVERIFIED sign convention of the exit delay as in 9i), the exit delay (J_exit,run minus J_exit,static) and the post-surge rate (mean r over 1 s and over 5 s after J_exit,run; both columns, none chosen, O12 ruling). For each metric: the median and the range over the seeds, the number of seeds for which it is defined and the number undefined, with the reasons. **Undefined entries are counted, never imputed.** No threshold is applied. The N rule above is evaluated here. Control classes are printed beside each N. The computations reuse the MS9 9i pieces (`surge_metrics.onset_delay`, `j_exit_metrics`, `ms9_9i.j_exit_static_path`).

## S10 and RB8, heterogeneity part (R8, E-7, E-8)

Gaussian and uniform must each give the Lorentzian network class in 3 of 3 seeds. The Lorentzian runs are the S10 Lorentzian runs (R3, separate records). **Comparator (E-7):** each Gaussian and uniform seed is compared with the Lorentzian reference only if the 3 Lorentzian seeds share one Table 2.1 correspondence class; otherwise RB8 is reported "NOT EVALUABLE" with the per-seed table (class and correspondence level of each Lorentzian, Gaussian and uniform seed) and no pass/fail. **Equality (E-8):** "gives the class" is read at the Table 2.1 correspondence level, which has three levels: surge-class membership (S+, S-rel, S-abs), S-sus, NS; the exact labels are reported beside. A failed run matches nothing. The class of each shape against the mean-field class and the control classes (C2, the Lorentzian one is the N = 1e4, 60 s control) are printed beside. The post-hoc count (S-rel not counted as a surge) is printed. M4 and adaptation parts of RB8 are MS12.

## G4 (R24, E-9)

Report-only for the run order. The network misses the surge at an N when no pooled seed at that N is in a surge class (S+, S-rel, S-abs; primary reading); the post-hoc count without S-rel is printed. **Pools (E-9):** "the network at N = 1e4" pools the Lorentzian records at N = 1e4 from S8 (the 6 headline runs), S9 (N = 1e4) and S10 (Lorentzian); "the network at N = 3e4" is S9 at 3e4. S8 cell runs, Gaussian and uniform runs and S9 at 1e3 and 3e3 are not pooled. Per-list counts are reported beside the pooled count. A pool with a missing record and no surge seed among the present ones is reported "not evaluable", never as a miss. The text of the three consequences of Tables 4.2 and 11.2 is produced with the truth of each condition: (1) misses at N = 1e4 and 3e4 gives a finite-size limitation and the model is kept; (2) disagreement in 2 or more of the 4 cells gives a rerun of RB6, whose network parts are S13 runs at MS11 (R9); (3) the affected results are reported as mean-field-only (our reading, UNVERIFIED: applies when (1) or (2) holds). If RB1 passes, G4 is not triggered.

Also stated as facts: the evaluators use the classifier's own f_pk (Table 2.1) for the network; the cell verdict ignores f_pk and onset; a median and a range over seeds are the only aggregation in S9; no multiple-comparison or confidence statement is made. Tolerance tests carry a numerical slack of 1e-9 (floating-point comparison of values such as exactly 5 percent), which is not a tolerance of the criteria.
