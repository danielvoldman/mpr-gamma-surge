Status: written at 11b (DL-343, DL-344); the 14 required fields that were open at 11b are ruled at 11c (DL-346 to DL-359, the reviewer's rulings 1 to 14 of the 11c prompt) and the rules are frozen at 11c (hashes in DL-364). This file adds no criterion, changes no threshold and reopens no ruling. Thresholds are read from the tag's blob by the evaluators and are not typed in `configs/ms11/ms11_eval.yaml` (a test checks this). The evaluators are `src/mprsurge/analysis/ms11_eval.py` and `scripts/run_A11.py`; at 11c they are tested on synthetic fixtures only and have not been run on any MS11 record or on the stored map series. ## 0. What every MS11 evaluator does first

1. The criteria loader gate (tag `criteria-frozen`) must verify; the thresholds are read from the tag's blob.
2. Any MS11 data file (the four list definition files, the list-hash file, the four run configs) that is not FROZEN, and any required field of `ms11_eval.yaml` that is open, stops the evaluation.
3. Records are looked up by the rows of the frozen lists (`configs/ms11/ms11_list_hashes.yaml`), by name. A record is refused if its git dirty flag is not exactly false, if it is a dry-run record, if its full config hash or name differs from the frozen row, or if its criteria SHA-256 is not the frozen one. A listed record that is absent is reported missing and every part that needs it is INCOMPLETE; it is never replaced. A record whose status is not ok is class `failed` (A9, never NS).
4. Labels attached to every output: "sigma checked, not confirmed" (G4-C4, sigma 0.0624) on every noisy mean-field result; "provisional (D8 open, DL-137)" on every class that went through a D8 call; the short-window false-surge rates per W (306, 400, 172, 24 of 500 at W = 0.2, 0.333, 0.593, 1.054 s; frozen criteria) beside the S11 classes at T_ramp 10 s and 30 s; G4-C1 to G4-C6, the MS9 and MS8 limits beside every output that uses a network or noisy record. Exploratory and post-hoc readings are labelled "post hoc", are kept out of the four pre-registered lists and feed no gate.
5. Time origins. The mean-field mode-N classes of S11 use the classifier onset (window centre, frozen O1); the raw crossing is secondary and post hoc. For the network part of S13 the time-origin check of MS10 (R27) is applied before any network record is read: same path clock, same `t_on`, same sample interval; a mismatch stops the evaluation.

**Comparison rule (ruling 5, `within_boundary` = inclusive, DL-350).** "Within X" in the plan is inclusive (at most X): RB9(a) "within 0.5 percent", RB9(b) "within 1/W", RB6 solver onset "within 1 ms". "<" and ">" are strict, as the plan writes them: RB6 "< 5%", "< 2%". ">=" and "<=" are inclusive: RB2 "at least 8 of 10", RB9(c) "<= 5%", RB10 "<= 10%". Implemented once, in `ms11_eval.compare`, with a tolerance of 1e-9 on the compared number; three boundary cases are tested (equality passes for "within", fails for "<").

## 1. RB2: S11 and the DL-251 controls (Table 7.2 RB2; DL-336 ruling 2b)

*Data.* MS11_S11: sigma factors 0.5, 1, 2 (sigma = factor x 0.0624) x T_ramp 10, 30, 120, 300 s x 10 seeds, P2 headline example, mode N. The seed index is the same noise stream at every sigma and T_ramp (the comparison across sigma is paired).

*Gate.* The headline surge class is "kept" in a run when its Table 2.1 class is a surge class: S+, S-rel or S-abs (ruled; `rb2_gate_class_set`). The test is the frozen "at least 8 of 10 seeds" at 0.5 sigma and at sigma, judged at the headline T_ramp 120 s (`rb2_judged_T_ramp_s`, the M11-2 recommendation, accepted; the plan names no T_ramp for the S11 test: UNVERIFIED reading). The count is the number of the 10 seeds in a surge class, the comparison is "at least" (the plan's text). Only these two sigma levels carry a verdict.

*Reported beside the gate, no verdict, feeding no gate.* (a) 2 sigma at every T_ramp (the frozen file: "2 sigma reported, no pass rule"); (b) the other T_ramp 10, 30, 300 s at every sigma; (c) the stricter readings S+ only and S+ or S-abs, labelled post hoc (the first of these not counting S-rel, as DL-250); (d) the class table per (sigma, T_ramp): counts per class, the metrics of Table 2.1 (median and range over seeds, undefined entries counted and not imputed).

*Controls (DL-251, POST-HOC AMENDMENT).* MS11_CTRL: constant parameters at the headline start state, headline record structure (2 s discard, 5 s baseline, 120 s ramp period, 10 s hold), the same three sigma factors, 10 seeds. The class rates of the controls are printed beside the S11 rates at T_ramp 120 s with the label "POST-HOC AMENDMENT (DL-251)"; they feed no gate. Reading: where the control also gives a surge class in k of 10 seeds, the S11 count at that sigma is not ramp evidence beyond the control (S-rel without a ramp occurred in 1,095 of 2,250 runs at MS9, DL-248, DL-250).

*Subgrid.* The noisy-subgrid agreement of RB2 is carried in as recorded at MS9: 97 of 225 cells (43.1 percent), 203 needed (90 percent, D24), FAILED (DL-242; P-1 post hoc 173 of 225, DL-255, DL-257). It is not re-evaluated.

*Ruled (ruling 1, DL-346):* a failed run among the 10 seeds counts as NOT kept; the denominator stays 10; the number of failed runs is reported with the count. A listed record that is absent is not a failed run: that cell is NOT EVALUABLE and nothing replaces it.

## 3. RB6 (numerical convergence), the parts open at MS11

*Mean field, noise-free.* rtol 1e-8 and 1e-12 against the 1e-10 grid run at T_ramp 10 s and 316.2278 s: the onset difference and the class are reported against rtol, not a pass test (plan). *Mean field, D43 floor.* Three floor levels x 10 seeds at T_ramp 10 s and 316.2278 s at the default Heun rung 1e-3 tau only (coupled increments at 5e-5 tau). The finest rung is not run there, so the 5 percent Heun test (default step against the finest rung) cannot be formed at these T_ramp; it stands at 120 s as recorded at MS8 (DL-184, DL-190: relative change 0, a limit of the 1 ms output grid). No verdict.

*Network (MS11_S13_NET).* N = 1e4, 10 s ramp, P2 headline, 3 seeds per configuration, six configurations: dt 1e-3, 5e-4, 2.5e-4, 1e-4 at V_p 100 and V_p 200 at dt 1e-3 and 1e-4. The configuration of dt 1e-3, V_p 100 is re-run under the new key; the MS10 S8 runs are not reused. For each seed and configuration, f_pk is read two ways (`rb6_network_fpk_sources`, ruled M11-6 (c), UNVERIFIED reading): (i) the classifier's episode f_pk (Table 2.1, resolution 1/W), (ii) the argmax of the PSD of v in 25 to 100 Hz (O6, resolution 1/W, no interpolation) over the window of the classifier episode of the same seed's dt 1e-3 run. The criterion "halving dt changes f_pk by less than 2 percent" is applied to the pair dt 1e-3 against dt 5e-4 at V_p 100 (frozen F-21), per seed, strictly less than the frozen 2 percent (the plan's "<", `rb6_network_fpk_bound_boundary`), for each of the two readings; the other rungs (5e-4 against 2.5e-4, 2.5e-4 against 1e-4) and the V_p pair are printed with their differences. No class claim is made from these runs (G4-C3 stands; no new controls); the classes are printed as information with that label. The network RB6 result is the network part of S13 as read in G4-C6 (UNVERIFIED reading).

*Ruled (ruling 3, DL-348):* a seed with no episode at dt 1e-3 has no f_pk; it is reported as NOT EVALUABLE, listed by seed, and neither passes nor fails the 2 percent bound. If changing dt creates or removes an episode, that is reported as a class change (the seed is then also NOT EVALUABLE for the bound: the f_pk at one of the two dt is undefined; UNVERIFIED reading). Verdict per f_pk reading: FAIL if any evaluable seed fails, PASS if at least one passes and none fails, NOT EVALUABLE if none is evaluable (UNVERIFIED reading: all evaluable seeds must be inside); the network row is FAIL if either reading fails, PASS if both pass, otherwise NOT EVALUABLE.
*Ruled (ruling 4, DL-349):* V_p +-100 against +-200 is REPORTED ONLY (the plan gives no pass number for it); the f_pk difference is shown as information, not as a test. The other dt rungs (5e-4 against 2.5e-4, 2.5e-4 against 1e-4) are printed the same way. The time-origin check (R27) runs on the network rows against the stored 10 s mean-field record before any network record is read.

## 4. RB9 (frequency mapping), stored series only except (d)

*(a) Dimensional against non-dimensional, within 0.5 percent.* The existing test-level check is described in DL-341. *Ruled (ruling 6, DL-351):* the existing unit-level test counts as the RB9(a) evidence, with its scope stated in the result as a limit: a 1 s path_eta ramp (eta 1 to 4), tau 10 and 20 ms, not the headline path, no run record. The same computation (`src/mprsurge/analysis/rb9a_unit.py`, which `tests/test_units.py` now calls) is run once as an analysis step by `scripts/run_rb9a_unit.py`, which stores the measured maximum relative difference in the labelled file `results/A11/rb9a_unit_level.json`; the value is cited from that file. No new RB9(a) run. "Within 0.5 percent" is inclusive (the test itself asserts strictly below; the A11 comparison follows ruling 5).

*(b) Spectral against cycle frequency, within 1/W.* Spectral f_pk is the PSD argmax in G (O6). Cycle frequency is the unwrapped analytic phase over 2 pi (O5, mode D). W is the window of the cell's T_ramp (D4), so the bound is 1/W per cell. *Ruled (rulings 7 and 8, DL-352, DL-353):* the gate is the headline run: the cycle frequency is computed from the stored v (unwrapped analytic phase of the 25 to 100 Hz band-passed v between the onset and the end of the classifier\'s flagged episode, over 2 pi, per second) and the spectral frequency is the stored classifier output (the episode f_pk, resolution 1/W); W is the classifier\'s spectrogram window length as frozen for the run\'s T_ramp (2 s at the headline, so the bound is 0.5 Hz); the comparison window is the classifier\'s flagged episode window per run (onset to end). Map 1 and map 2 cells that have a flagged episode and a stored v series are REPORTED as the fraction within 1/W; no gate fraction is invented.

*(c) Band 25 to 100 Hz against 30 to 120 Hz, at most 5 percent of cells change class.* The frozen A8 band variant (band_30_120, `a8_sensitivity.classify`) is applied to the stored mode-D series of map 1 (900 cells, 165,001 samples each) and map 2 (325 cells, 25 cells x 13 T_ramp), mode D. A cell "changes class" when its raw Table 2.1 class differs from the stored baseline class (`results/ms9/9e/map1_classes.csv`, `9f/map2_classes.csv`); the three-category change is printed beside (ruled M11-7). The noisy subgrid (2,250 mode-N runs) is reported without a verdict. *Ruled (ruling 9, DL-354):* map 1 (900 cells) carries the RB9(c) and RB10 verdicts; map 2 (325 cells) and the noisy subgrid are reported beside it and feed no verdict (status REPORTED, with the status they would have had printed as information). *Ruled (ruling 10, DL-355):* cells where a variant does not fit the stored record (class `not_run`, as at DL-147), or whose stored baseline is not a class, are excluded from numerator and denominator; the verdict states the evaluated denominator ("k of n evaluated cells, m excluded"); the worst case (every excluded cell counted as changed, over all cells) is printed beside it; if the two give different pass or fail results the verdict is flagged "depends on exclusions". If no cell is evaluable the verdict is NOT EVALUABLE.

*(d) tau plus or minus 20 percent, reported only.* Ruled M11-8 (d): two mean-field runs at tau = 8 and 12 ms on the headline path, reported only. *Ruled (ruling 12, DL-357):* YES, a small separate list MS11_RB9D with the two runs, reported only, in no gate. **STOPPED: the parameter specification (what is held fixed and what changes) is ambiguous in the plan and in the code; the list is not written and the question is with the reviewer (DL-357, report to the reviewer).** The runs are deterministic mean-field runs (kind `mf`, `seeds.spawn_key` null in `configs/base.yaml`), so they use no noise stream and need no new seed key; the first element 11 of the key family is untouched. A11 shows the RB9(d) row as NOT EVALUABLE ("runs not specified yet") until the list has run.

## 7. A11 (`scripts/run_A11.py`, not written at 11b)

Rows: one per criterion leaf of the frozen file (RB1 to RB10 leaves, the gates' leaves that are RB-derived), `a11_row_unit = criterion_leaf`. Columns (rulings 13 and 14, DL-358, DL-359): value, threshold, pass_or_fail, input_commits (the plan), then rb_id, check_id, source (MS and DL reference), logged_verdict (the logged word verbatim), recomputed_match (yes or no), n_evaluated, label (pre-registered or post hoc), and `note` (an addition at 11c, not in the ruling, UNVERIFIED: it says whether a row was recomputed, has no logged verdict yet, or carries a flag such as "depends on exclusions"). Status words (ruling 13): PASS, FAIL, NOT EVALUABLE, REPORTED. Every logged verdict word maps to one of them through the table `a11_logged_verdict_map` in `ms11_eval.yaml` (PASS, FAIL, FAILED, MEETS, DOES NOT MEET, NOT EVALUABLE, NOT INSIDE, NOT JUDGED, NO VERDICT, NOT IN MS8 RUNS, OPEN, REPORTED, PASSED, PASSED, continue, CONTINUE, PASSED WITH DEPARTURES, NOT PASSED); a logged word with no entry raises `UnmappedVerdict`. The table is the implementer\'s: the ruling gave the rule, not the entries; the mappings NOT INSIDE to FAIL, CONTINUE and PASSED WITH DEPARTURES to PASS, and the M4 part OPEN to NOT EVALUABLE are UNVERIFIED readings for the reviewer to confirm. The 11b words NOT_JUDGED and OPEN are no longer status words. Mode: each check evaluated at MS8, MS9 and MS10 is recomputed from the stored records with the stored evaluators and compared with the logged verdict; the logged verdict is printed beside; any difference is flagged and reported, never resolved silently. Failed checks (RB1 FAIL, RB2 subgrid FAILED, RB6 solver clause DOES NOT MEET, RB3(a) not judged at the headline, RB3(b) under both onset readings, RB8 heterogeneity NOT EVALUABLE) appear as logged and are never shown as PASS, never dropped. No new numeric threshold is written into A11. The carried checks of MS8 to MS10 are registered in `ms11_eval.yaml` (`a11_logged`, 26 rows, each logged word verbatim with its source). They are NOT recomputed by A11 at 11c: `assemble_rows` has a recompute-hook interface, but no hook is registered, so their `recomputed_match` is "no" and the note says "not recomputed". Wiring the stored MS8 to MS10 evaluators as hooks is a later step; until then the M11-10 (c) "recompute and compare" holds for the MS11 checks only, and an MS11 check has no logged verdict at its first evaluation (`recomputed_match` "no", note "no logged verdict yet").
