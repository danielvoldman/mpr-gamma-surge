# MS4 sub-step plan (planning only; no ruling yet, three questions open)

MS4 plan, written before any MS4 code. Binding source: `docs/plan.md` (Table 11.1 row MS4; sections 5.2 A3 and A2, 4.3 gate G1, Table 6.2 row S2, Table 2.1 and D8, CP17 and CP26, Table 9.1). Decisions go in `docs/decision_log.md`; this plan is DL-106 and holds none of its own. It changes nothing in the plan. Rules as for MS2 and MS3: one sub-step at a time; after each, `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest -m "not slow"` green, one commit `<type>(<scope>): <summary> [IDs]`, a report of at most 8 lines, then stop. No tags, no pushes unless asked. Every run writes a provenance record (6.6). Drivers only integrate, analysis modules only analyse, figure scripts only plot (10.1). Nothing is run in 4a.

**MS4 row (Table 11.1):** "M2 ramp diagnostic: S2, A3". Gate: "Slowest ramp jumps within one step of the saddle-node; G1 metric (envelope growth) evaluated". Unlocks: G1, then MS5. Depends on MS2 (A1, S1, K5) and MS3 (S2 allowance), both done.

**Goal as the plan states it:** S2 runs M2 mean-field ramps at J/sqrt(Delta) = 15 with eta_bar from 1 below the lower fold to 1 above the upper fold (A1), 13 T_ramp values 1 to 1000 s (log). A3 records jump time, eta_bar at the jump and ring class versus T_ramp. A3 check: the slowest ramp jumps within one step of the A1 saddle-node value, and K5 bursting is reproduced in S1. G1 (section 4.3, after A1 to A3, S1, S2): stay with M2 only if some path with Delta > 0 and tau = 10 ms gives a non-damped episode in G over >= 10 cycles (D8: fitted envelope growth not negative by more than 2 standard errors; for M2 the fit window starts at the first of the cycles counted in G, SC-06); otherwise move to M3. The move is expected (reasoned, not tested). MS4 evaluates the G1 metric; the decision to move is the user's at the gate.

## Open items that touch MS4

1. **"Within one step" of the saddle-node (A3 check, Table 11.1 gate).** The plan does not say whether a step is an A1 grid step (0.05 in eta_bar/Delta at base spacing, 0.025 at half spacing near the fold), the eta_bar change per output sample, or something else. Needed from the user: which one. Default if no ruling: the A1 half-spacing step (0.025 in eta_bar/Delta, equal to 0.025 in eta_bar at Delta = 1), reported next to the offset in eta_bar and in output samples; UNVERIFIED. Also needed: which fold is the target. The ramp rises from fold_low - 1 to fold_up + 1; reasoned (not tested) that the slow ramp jumps up at the upper fold at -3.136; A3 reads the fold from A1 for the sweep direction rather than assuming it.
2. **K5 definition.** K5 passes at MS2 on a definition that is ours and UNVERIFIED (DL-064, DL-082, DL-088): burst = crossing of the midpoint of the I = 0 node and focus, tolerance 0.1 tau, period 40 tau, 2 transient + 5 checked periods, source MPR Fig. 2 (single source). It is not redefined here and not re-run. The A3 check names "K5 bursting reproduced in S1" and MS4 cites the MS2 result. Needed from the user only if you want a different definition or a source check before G1: the definition and a ruling to re-run K5 (that would also touch the frozen `replication.yaml`, hence the tag `tolerances-frozen`).
3. **S2 run design not in the plan.** Baseline time before the ramp, hold after it, output sampling interval and the profile (linear assumed as the S2 default; the S5 profile pair {linear, sigmoid} is not named for S2), and the G band for M2 at tau = 10 ms. The ring frequency at the K4 point is about 92 Hz (fit omega 5.77 per tau, tau 10 ms), inside the base-config primary band 25 to 100 Hz but near its upper edge, so ring frequencies at other eta_bar may leave it; the plan's S2 row gives no band for M2: UNVERIFIED. Default for 4b: reuse `analysis.band` of `base.yaml` and the sampling of the timing cell, logged. Needed from the user only if the defaults are not acceptable.
4. **G1 scope.** G1 asks about "some path with Delta > 0, tau = 10 ms". S2 supplies one parameter point (J = 15, Delta = 1). MS4 evaluates the metric on S2 and states that this is one path. Adding other M2 points (other J, Delta, I paths) is a scope change for the user to rule.
5. **Growth estimator caveats (DL-062, DL-073, DL-093).** The plain OLS SE is too small, so a "damped" call may come too easily; the step-onset bias distorts the rate magnitude, not the sign, in the case tried. DL-083 (2) deferred the fix to MS7 and keeps the strict xfail. G1 uses sign and the 2-SE test, so MS4 runs on the current estimator and the gate report states the caveat beside every G1 row. A G1 verdict that flips under the caveat would need a user ruling before the fix is moved up; this is not decided here.
6. **Map 1 Delta spacing (linear, UNVERIFIED).** `timing.map_grid` takes Delta linear and tau_d log; the plan names Delta 0.01 to 0.6 (30) without a spacing. It concerns S7 (MS8), not MS4, and only enters the MS3 timing figure (4.44 h at 10 workers, recomputed at the start of 4a from the raw files in `results/timing/clean/`: 4.442 h, matching `docs/ms3_gate_report.md`). The spacing is for the user at the MS8 plan; if it changes to log, the timing estimate of map 1 should be re-run.
7. **Carried from MS3 (DL-105):** the Heun loaded-rate item and the unjudged D36 cap are MS5 items, not MS4.
8. **Fast tests reading git-ignored results/ (DL-092 (1)):** MS4 tests should use tmp_path fixtures, not `results/`; the old tests are not fixed here.
