- **A5** (5.2): periodic-orbit continuation from the A4 Hopf points (eta_bar at ref_devalle; tau_d at ref_ceni): amplitude, period, stability, folds of cycles, criticality. Output A_ref. Feeds `results/A5/a5_cycles.npz`, F2.
- **S4** (6.2): M3 mean field quasi-static sweeps, eta_bar from eta_H - 2 to eta_H + 4, 61 steps up then down, 2 s each (1 s discarded); network at 15 steps. Feeds A5, F2.

Check: K10 in the Ceni regime (5 to 30 Hz, falling with tau_d, flat in J); band G at ref_devalle (K8). Feeds `results/A6/a6_freq_map.npz`, G2, F3. Resolves C2.
- **What feeds from MS6:** G2 (4.3 / Table 11.2: move to M4 if (a) no limit cycle >= 25 Hz with Delta below critical within Table 3.2 ranges, (b) no eta_bar path enters the oscillatory region from a stable focus, (c) the interneuron-drive assumption is rejected; expected: stay, M4 as robustness only); the D40 confirmation (headline P2, ref_devalle, T_ramp 120 s, linear, I = 0, confirmed after A6 and before any M3 ramp-study run, S5 onward); MS7.
