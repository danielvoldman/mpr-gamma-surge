# 13t-1f: fresh-subagent review of the SHORT captions of the recorded build from e4780c3 (word for word as received; the harness indentation of the hand-back is removed)

Brief given: read each SHORT caption alone, as a reader of the journal who has never seen the project, with the image next to it; list every sentence or term you could not understand, every statement the image does not support, and every text on an image that looks like an internal code. Report only.

Method note: I viewed the four third-size images and the four captions. I did not open the 16 crop files, so very small text was judged at third size only.
